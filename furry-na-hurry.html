Got Questions?

We've Got Answers!

How long does a mobile grooming appointment take?
Most appointments take 1–2.5 hours depending on size and coat condition. Small dogs can be done in 60–90 minutes. We'll give you an honest estimate at booking.
Do I need to stay home during the appointment?
Yes — we ask that an adult is available for the first appointment. For regular returning clients, many owners are comfortable letting us work in the driveway while they're inside. We text you when done!
What's included in a full groom?
A full groom includes: shampoo and conditioning, blow dry, full breed-style haircut, nail trim and file, ear cleaning, teeth brushing, and a bandana. Your dog gets the full works!
My dog is anxious — can you handle that?
Absolutely — this is one of the main reasons people choose mobile grooming! One-on-one, calm environment, familiar surroundings. We're anxiety-certified and have groomed dogs other groomers refused. Just let us know at booking.
How do I book an appointment?
Call or text us at (725) 250-7711 — we reply within the hour. We'll ask about your dog's breed, size, and preferred services, then lock in a time that works for you.
