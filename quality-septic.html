Don't Ignore These

Signs Your Tank Needs Service Now

Catch problems early — before they become expensive emergencies.

Slow Drains Throughout the House

If every drain is slow — not just one fixture — your tank may be full or the drain field overwhelmed.

Foul Odors Near the Tank

Sewage smells in your yard or inside the house signal a system that needs immediate attention.

Wet or Mushy Spots in Yard

Soggy ground over your drain field — even without rain — means effluent is surfacing. Call right away.

Gurgling Sounds in Pipes

Unusual bubbling from toilets or sinks is a common early warning sign of septic trouble.

Sewage Backup

Any sewage backing up into tubs, toilets, or floor drains is a health emergency. Call immediately.

Unusually Lush Grass

Bright green, fast-growing grass over your drain field means it's receiving too much effluent. A sure sign.
