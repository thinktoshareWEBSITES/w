Don't Ignore These

Warning Signs Your System Needs Help

Catching problems early saves thousands. Call us if you notice any of these.

Slow Drains Everywhere

When every fixture drains slowly at once, it's likely a full tank or main line blockage — not just a clogged pipe.

Sewage Smell Indoors

Foul odors near drains or in the basement mean gases are backing up through your system.

Soggy Ground Over Tank

Unusually green or wet patches above your drain field mean effluent is surfacing — a sign of system failure.

Gurgling Toilets

Gurgling after flushing often indicates a blocked vent or an overfull tank — don't wait to get it checked.

It's Been 3+ Years

Most tanks need pumping every 3–5 years. If it's been longer, you're overdue — risking a very expensive backup.

Sewage Near the Tank

Any pooling, dark staining, or strong odors near your buried tank needs professional attention right away.

The Minnesota Pollution Control Agency issues certifications for operators who handle and land-apply bio-solids. Grade 4 is the highest level, requiring state exams and strict environmental compliance. It means every pump-out we do follows proper protocols from pickup to final disposal — most companies in the area don't hold this certification.

Most residential pump-outs take 1–2 hours from arrival to cleanup. Larger tanks, difficult access, or significant buildup may take longer. We'll give you a time estimate when you call.
