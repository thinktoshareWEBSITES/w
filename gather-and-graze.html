About Emma

The Woman Behind
Every Board

Hi, I'm Emma -- a self-taught charcuterie artist and food lover who turned a passion for beautiful, delicious gatherings into a business. I started Gather & Graze because I believe every occasion deserves a stunning centerpiece.

I source my cheeses from local artisan dairies, my meats from trusted butchers, and my produce seasonally. Every board is assembled fresh the morning of your event -- no pre-made platters, no shortcuts.

Whether it's a romantic evening for two or a 200-person wedding -- I put the same care and creativity into every single board.
