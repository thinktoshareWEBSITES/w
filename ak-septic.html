Don’t Ignore These

Signs Your Septic Needs Attention Now

Catching problems early saves thousands. If you notice any of these, call us today.

Slow Drains Throughout the House

When every drain runs slow — not just one — your tank is likely full or the drain field is overwhelmed.

Foul Odors in the Yard or House

Sewage smells near your tank or inside are a clear signal something is wrong. Don’t wait on this one.

Wet or Soggy Spots in the Yard

Soggy ground over your drain field — even with no rain — means effluent is surfacing. Call right away.

Gurgling Sounds from Toilets

Bubbling or gurgling from your toilets or sinks after flushing is an early warning sign. Don’t ignore it.

Sewage Backup Inside — EMERGENCY

Raw sewage backing up into your drains or toilets is a health hazard. Call us immediately — we respond 24/7.

Unusually Lush Grass Over the Field

Bright green, fast-growing grass above your drain field is a sign it’s receiving too much effluent.
