Warning Signs

Is Your Lawn Sending You a Signal?

These are the signs your lawn is suffering - and costing you money. Catch them early before they become expensive problems.

Brown or Bare Patches

Irregular dry spots signal fungal disease, grub damage, or drainage issues that worsen without treatment. Act fast.

Weeds Spreading Fast

Dandelions, crabgrass, and clover taking over means your lawn's turf density is too low to choke them out naturally.

Spongy or Soggy Soil

Ground that stays wet or bounces underfoot means thatch buildup or drainage problems that can kill grass roots.

Grass Growing Unevenly

Wild, patchy growth with some areas too thick and others thin suggests soil compaction and lack of nutrients.

Overgrown & Untamed Edges

Grass creeping over driveways and walkways signals missed maintenance that drops curb appeal and can invite pests.

Moss Growing in Lawn

Moss patches mean compacted, acidic, or shaded soil with poor drainage - a sign the lawn needs professional aeration.
